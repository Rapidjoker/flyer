About The Developers

RIC DEVELOPMENT (PTY) Ltd is an innovative property development enterprise established by a group of investors based in Africa.

Promoting a community of fine living and comfort, Sarona City is created for those welcoming a lifestyle full of energy and excitement with all required amenities within a 2-kilometer radius. Pick 'n Pay Hyper, fashion stores, ATM machines, Engen service station, preschool, and luxurious accommodation are all within walking distance, surrounded by lovely landscaped walkways.

  • ✦ Tenant Mix: Fine dining, diverse fashion, hair & beauty, pharmaceuticals, groceries, stationery, laundromat, and a dedicated medical centre.
